Format and how to write: "Verify and close" section of `SKILL.md`. Read only the lessons (`Read` with `limit: 30`); the log is searched with `Grep`. Lines before 2026-11-09: `scoreboard-archive.md`. Figures per model and effort: `node ~/.claude/hooks/telemetria.mjs --resumo`.

## Lessons

- Cost is execution time: each turn re-reads the context. On 2026-11-09/10, per subagent: Fable high of 64 min, $15.41 (n=10); `implementer` Sonnet high of 100 min, $3.99 (n=45); Opus high of 26 min, $3.45 (n=49); Haiku, $0.02 to $0.06. An expensive subagent only with a short scope; the conductor was 72% of the cost over 30 days. (telemetry, n=3,103 and n≈330)
- What brings Haiku down is its own reasoning and a big packet, not reading: high effort, one Edit per section and reading in short ranges ended the drops; as a juror, it dropped out in 5 of 5 rounds with a 300-thousand-character packet. (n≈15)
- Small units win: parts of 3 to 5 items with a call cap solved every broad task that failed; a 300-line digest given to a single Haiku came back with 1 of 16 sections. Fan-out through Workflow fails less than an agent launched directly. (n≈13)
- Give the worker the ready table to "confirm or correct" and ask for the literal excerpt as proof; open search yields less. "Doesn't exist" from someone who only checked the git log is often false: require a Grep in the code. (n≈7)
- "Passed" needs proof. A new test that does not fail without the fix showed up 3 times (invalid grep, only week 1, a line swapped for nothing): prove it by mutation. A worker that says done before its own oracle finishes is not done: ask for the command in the foreground and the last line. (n=6)
- Review pays: the reviewer found a blocker or must-fix in 7 of 13 uses, and the round after the fix found something new in 2 of 2. The devil's advocate upheld an objection in 7 of 7, one of them a blocker that a 6 to 0 council did not see, because the juror judges the packet and the packet lacked the measurement. (n=20)
- The quick council decides: 38 of 43 closed. The 5 that did not pay off were a false premise in the packet (2), a packet overwritten by a participant (the script now protects it) and divergence (2). Check at the source every "the repo has no X" before building the packet. (n=43)
- Hill climb by score does not reach the target: 0 of 7 with a goal of average 9 reached 9 (posts 0 of 61 approved, skill stuck at 8.2, definitions blocked for 3 to 5 rounds). The gain comes in the first round and the rest is noise. Accept by blind vote against the previous version, with a cap of 3 rounds, and use the score only to find the lowest category. (n=7)
- The absolute score of a media jury does not order versions: it stayed between 5.7 and 6.1 across three versions that the blind vote separated 8 to 1 and 9 to 0; the same version varied 0.76 between juries; the decision scale comes out about 1 point above the scores mode's. With 4 sheets the vote spreads; the face-off of 2 separates. (n=3 tracks)
- Art: deriving from a base (vectorized version, similar rig) beats drawing from scratch (2 of 2), and a local fix with the structure fixed beats changing the structure (3 of 3). Check the asset's use in the game before generating: $3.60 went to targets excluded later. (n=6)
- Whether an outsider understands is measured with a binary cold reader (Haiku with no context, an answer key, up to 2 rewrites), not with a jury score. (n=1 routine, council decision 8 to 0)
- Coverage A/B: different angles (official x community, code x docs, grep x reading) find different things; keep the union and check at the source what matters. Selection A/B: the variant that starts from the cause beats the one that patches the symptom. (n≈20)
- A PC resource with a counted slot (Godot) queues behind a call that blocks; a timing test that fails on a loaded machine is load until proven otherwise; a UI task with a test on a loaded machine goes straight to the conductor. (owner's rule of 2026-11-09; a train failed at 3933 of 3936 and the same PRs passed with the machine at load 3)
- A paid source (ABNT standard) is not searched on the web: ask the owner for the PDF before launching researchers. Some agent types refuse to write a .md report: ask for .txt. (n=2)
